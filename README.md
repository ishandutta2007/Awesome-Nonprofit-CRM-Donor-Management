# Awesome-Nonprofit-CRM-Donor-Management

## Top Nonprofit CRM & Donor Management Tools Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Donor Stewardship, Fundraising Automation & Constituent Relationship Intelligence*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Nonprofit CRM & Donor Management Tools**. These tools help nonprofits manage donor relationships, track gifts and pledges, automate fundraising campaigns, coordinate volunteers, process online donations, and generate impact reports—all from a unified constituent database.

**Examples** include Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud, Blackbaud Raiser's Edge NXT, Bloomerang, DonorPerfect, Neon CRM, Virtuous, Givebutter, Classy, Little Green Light, and Kindful (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom donor workflows, and transparent constituent data management — ideal for nonprofits, NGOs, and developers building data-sovereign fundraising platforms.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud](https://www.salesforce.com/nonprofit/)**  
  Enterprise CRM for nonprofits with donor management, grantmaking, program management, and marketing automation.

- **[Blackbaud Raiser's Edge NXT](https://www.blackbaud.com/products/blackbaud-raisers-edge-nxt)**  
  Comprehensive fundraising and donor management platform for large nonprofits and educational institutions.

- **[Bloomerang](https://bloomerang.com/)**  
  Donor management software with retention-focused analytics and donor engagement scoring for small to mid-size nonprofits.

- **[DonorPerfect](https://www.donorperfect.com/)**  
  Flexible donor management and fundraising software with online donation forms, receipts, and reporting.

- **[Neon CRM](https://neonone.com/)**  
  Nonprofit CRM with membership management, fundraising, events, and email marketing in one platform.

- **[Virtuous](https://virtuous.org/)**  
  Responsive fundraising CRM with donor behavior insights, AI-powered recommendations, and automation.

- **[Givebutter](https://givebutter.com/)**  
  Free fundraising platform with donation pages, events, auctions, and donor management tools.

- **[Classy](https://www.classy.org/)**  
  Online fundraising platform for nonprofits with peer-to-peer campaigns, recurring giving, and donor data management.

- **[Little Green Light](https://www.littlegreenlight.com/)**  
  Affordable donor management software for small nonprofits with customizable forms and integrations.

- **[Kindful](https://kindful.com/)**  
  Donor management and fundraising platform with CRM, online giving pages, and donor insights.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[CiviCRM](https://github.com/civicrm/civicrm-core)**  
  The #1 open-source CRM for nonprofits with donor management, membership, events, case management, and bulk email. Used by 10,000+ organizations worldwide with integrations for Stripe, PayPal, Mailchimp, Twilio, and more. AGPL v3 licensed. Available as self-hosted or managed hosting via CiviCRM Spark starting at $15/month. [citation:10][citation:13][citation:15]

- **[Houdini Project](https://github.com/HoudiniProject/houdini)**  
  Free and open-source fundraising infrastructure for nonprofits and NGOs with donation processing, campaign management, and Stripe integration. Ruby on Rails based with multi-tenant support. [citation:2]

- **[NationForge Community](https://github.com/programozas-kft/nationforge-community)**  
  Open-source CRM and community management platform for civic organizations built with Laravel 12 and Livewire. Features contact management, donations, events, projects, email campaigns, and role-based access. [citation:7]

- **[Odoo Nonprofit Donation Management](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/nonprofit_donation_mgmt_pro)**  
  Back-office donor, donation, campaign, grant, and impact management suite for nonprofits running on Odoo. Includes RFM segmentation, engagement scoring, recurring donations with Stripe/PayPal, tax receipts, and grant lifecycle tracking. [citation:4]

- **[Donation Platform for WooCommerce](https://github.com/wp-plugins/wc-donation-platform)**  
  WordPress plugin for fundraising and donation management with Stripe/PayPal integration, recurring donations, and donor portal. Ideal for nonprofits using WooCommerce. [citation:11]

- **[Mautic](https://github.com/mautic/mautic)**  
  Open-source marketing automation platform with email campaigns, lead scoring, and multi-channel follow-up. Integrates with CiviCRM for donor communications. [citation:1]

- **[EspoCRM](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm)**  
  Open-source CRM with customizable entities for donor management, contact tracking, and workflow automation. Self-hosted alternative to Salesforce for smaller organizations. [citation:1]

- **[SuiteCRM](https://github.com/salesagility/SuiteCRM)**  
  Open-source CRM fork of SugarCRM with modules adaptable for donor management, grant tracking, and constituent relationships.

- **[Pretix](https://github.com/pretix/pretix)**  
  Open-source event ticketing platform suitable for fundraising events with payment processing and attendee management. Recommended as a starter tool for small NGOs. [citation:1]

- **[LimeSurvey](https://github.com/LimeSurvey/LimeSurvey)**  
  Open-source survey platform for donor feedback, program evaluations, and community assessments. Low-dependency starter tool for nonprofits. [citation:1]

- **[Nextcloud](https://github.com/nextcloud/server)**  
  Open-source file collaboration and document management platform for storing donor documents, grant agreements, and program files securely. [citation:1]

- **[HumHub](https://github.com/humhub/humhub)**  
  Open-source social network and member community platform for nonprofit member engagement and supporter networking. [citation:1]

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **[Donazy](https://github.com/kodingworks/donazy)** — Open-source donation and fundraising platform with management features for community organizations. [citation:18]
- **[Catarse](https://github.com/catarse/catarse)** — Open-source crowdfunding platform with payment integrations, suitable for nonprofit campaigns. [citation:18]
- **[Grasruts](https://github.com/Grasruts/grasruts)** — Open-source crowdfunding platform for community fundraising initiatives. [citation:18]
- **[Bitrix24](https://github.com/bitrix24)** — Open-source self-hosted CRM with donor management capabilities and omnichannel contact center. [citation:6]
- **[Perfex CRM](https://github.com/perfexcrm)** — Open-source CRM adaptable for nonprofit donor management with customization. [citation:20]
- **Payment Processing Libraries** — Stripe, PayPal, and GoCardless open-source SDKs for custom donation processing.
- **Email Marketing Tools** — Mautic, phpList, and Listmonk for donor communications and newsletters.
- **Reporting & Analytics** — Metabase, Apache Superset, and Grafana for custom donor dashboards.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **CiviCRM** or **EspoCRM** for constituent management, **Odoo** for accounting integration, **Mautic** for email campaigns, **Pretix** for event ticketing, and **Metabase + PostgreSQL** for donor analytics to build a complete self-hosted nonprofit stack.
